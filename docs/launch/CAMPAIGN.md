# A 10× launch built on verifiable product use

**Objective:** outperform the mlxtop launch by 10× on a defined, comparable outcome. Virality cannot be promised. The campaign should produce successful trials and returning contributors as well as reach.

## Set a comparable baseline

The public mlxtop post was rechecked: 232 reactions and 15 comments were visible, including interest in Duet's architecture. These are snapshots, not attribution. The earlier launch brief records 50,000 impressions and 90 stars; those private figures were not independently verified in this pass.

Before day 0, record matching 14-day windows for mlxtop and Duet: impressions, unique repository visitors, net new stars and reported successful trials where available. Unknown values stay blank. If the earlier figures are confirmed for a comparable window, 10× would mean **500,000 impressions or 900 net new stars**, depending on the selected metric. It does not mean 10,000 stars; that was a different stretch target in the earlier brief.

Use repository visitors as the comparable acquisition measure where both baselines exist. Track successful synthetic trials as the product outcome. Count stars as an additional signal, not proof of deployment or recurring use. [GitHub traffic](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/viewing-activity-and-data-for-your-repository/viewing-traffic-to-a-repository) has a rolling 14-day window; save the aggregate data weekly. Do not add product telemetry merely to measure a launch.

## Four experiments, with proposed stop rules

These thresholds are planning decisions, not externally validated benchmarks.

1. **Hook:** product continuation (“the agent behind mlxtop”) versus a later audit finding. Keep subject and artifact aligned; compare qualified visits/trials per 1,000 impressions. Sequential posts are not a randomized causal test.
2. **Demo comprehension:** ask five independent testers to identify which model receives sensitive content and where to inspect the record. If fewer than four can, revise the caption/first screen before more distribution.
3. **First success:** after 20 voluntary trials, target at least 70% completion without maintainer help. Below that, prioritize onboarding and failures over promotional volume.
4. **Evaluation depth:** count outside reproductions, substantive issues and returning testers. If reach rises without these outcomes, narrow the audience and improve the task.

No paid distribution until first use works. Approach potential collaborators only when the owner chooses to, with a relevant runnable artifact. No outreach, replies or social posts have been sent.

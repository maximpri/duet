# Enterprise LLM privacy for banks: frontier labs and hyperscale clouds (state as of 2026-09-25)

*Conventions: "primary" means the vendor's own docs, terms or blog, fetched directly. Items marked [secondary] come from press or aggregator pages. Items marked [search-summary] were seen only in search-result summaries and were not fetched in full, so verify them before quoting. Model names (Claude Fable/Mythos 5.x, GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna, GPT-6 Astra, and so on) are as they appear in the 2026 sources.*

## 1. Data-use terms: training, retention, abuse monitoring, ZDR, and 2025-2026 changes

### Takeaway
All five vendors promise contractually that they will not train on enterprise or API data by default. They also all keep some data by default for abuse monitoring, usually up to 30 days. Zero data retention (ZDR) is gated: OpenAI and Anthropic require sales approval, and Azure and Google require an application form or a particular contract type. The big change in 2026 is that the most capable "frontier/covered/Advanced AI" models require retention again, typically 30 days and sometimes with human review, on every channel. Vendors are now competing to bring ZDR back for those models: OpenAI with Private Safety Processing (Aug–Sep 2026), and Anthropic with Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (announced Sep 2026). Separately, the NYT v. OpenAI preservation order (May–Sep 2025) showed that non-ZDR API data can be frozen by a court order.

### Cited Findings

#### OpenAI (API, ChatGPT Enterprise/Business/Edu)
- Business data from ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, Healthcare, Edu, Teachers and the API Platform (after March 1, 2023) is not used for training by default. Training happens only if the customer explicitly opts in, for example through feedback mechanisms — [OpenAI Enterprise privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/)
- API: "OpenAI may securely retain API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days to provide the services and to identify abuse. After 30 days, API inputs and outputs are removed from our systems, unless we are legally required to retain them" — [OpenAI Enterprise privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/); abuse-monitoring logs are kept "up to 30 days, unless longer retention is required by law" — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
- Access to API business data is limited to "(1) authorized employees that require access for engineering support, investigating potential platform abuse, and legal compliance and (2) specialized third-party contractors who are bound by confidentiality and security obligations, solely to review for abuse and misuse" — [OpenAI Enterprise privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/)
- All business data may go through automated content classifiers and safety tools. The resulting classifications are metadata that "do not contain any of the business data itself" — [OpenAI Enterprise privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/)
- ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare: "Your workspace admins control how long your data is retained. Any deleted conversations are removed from our systems within 30 days, unless we are legally required to retain them." ChatGPT Business adds "or is reasonably necessary to protect our services or any third party from harm" — [OpenAI Enterprise privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/)
- ZDR and Modified Abuse Monitoring (MAM) both keep customer content out of abuse-monitoring logs. ZDR also forces `store=false`. Both require "prior approval by OpenAI and acceptance of additional requirements" through sales — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
- ZDR-eligible endpoints: `/v1/chat/completions`, `/v1/responses`, `/v1/embeddings`, `/v1/images/*`, `/v1/audio/*`, `/v1/completions`, `/v1/realtime`, `/v1/moderations`. Not eligible: `/v1/conversations`, `/v1/assistants`, `/v1/threads`, `/v1/vector_stores`, `/v1/files`, `/v1/fine_tuning/jobs`, `/v1/agents`, `/v1/batches`, `/v1/evals`. Audio outputs are kept 1 hour; prompt cache lives in "GPU-local storage... not retained after the 24-hour expiration" — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
- ZDR has exceptions. Images flagged as potential CSAM "will be retained for manual review, even if Zero Data Retention... is enabled." Data sent to MCP servers "is subject to their data retention policies." Live web search is not HIPAA-eligible — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
- **2026 change: Private Safety Processing (PSP).** It was previewed on Aug 20, 2026. On Sep 22, 2026 OpenAI updated: "We are rolling out Private Safety Processing to API customers, with access expanding in phases. Private Safety Processing enables us to continue offering ZDR as frontier models become more capable." In ZDR deployments, "customer content remains on infrastructure the customer controls." Another option under development stores content on OpenAI infrastructure "encrypted with keys controlled by the customer. OpenAI personnel do not have a copy of those keys." When a risk is found, OpenAI "receives a narrowly defined signal", and personnel "do not receive access to the customer content even when it is flagged" — [OpenAI: Offering ZDR for frontier models](https://openai.com/index/offering-zero-data-retention-for-frontier-models/)
- OpenAI's post takes a swipe at the competition: "Some recent frontier-model deployments have required customers to allow their AI provider to retain sensitive content for safety monitoring. For many organizations, such requirements conflict with their security obligations" — [OpenAI: Offering ZDR for frontier models](https://openai.com/index/offering-zero-data-retention-for-frontier-models/). The Register contrasts the two approaches: Anthropic keeps covered-model prompts and outputs for 30 days and allows human review of flagged content, while OpenAI's human intervention is "limited to child exploitation detection only" — [The Register, 2026-08-20](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/20/openai-chases-anthropics-biz-customers-with-zero-data-retention-pledge/5290609) [secondary]
- **NYT v. OpenAI preservation order (2025).** The order covered ChatGPT Free/Plus/Pro/Team and API customers without a ZDR agreement. It "does not impact ChatGPT Enterprise or ChatGPT Edu customers" or "API customers who are using Zero Data Retention endpoints under our ZDR amendment." The court clarified on May 27 [2025] that Enterprise was excluded. Update of Oct 22, 2025: "Our obligations under the earlier order ended on September 26, 2025." OpenAI "will securely store limited historical April–September 2025 user data... accessible only to a small, audited OpenAI legal and security team" — [OpenAI: response to NYT data demands](https://openai.com/index/response-to-nyt-data-demands/)
- Magistrate Judge Ona T. Wang's Oct 9 [2025] order ended the obligation to "preserve and segregate all output log data that would otherwise be deleted on a going forward basis". Data tied to accounts the NYT flagged still has to be preserved — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/ai/openai-no-longer-has-to-preserve-all-of-its-chatgpt-data-with-some-exceptions-192422093.html) [secondary, search-summary]
- On Jan 5, 2026, District Judge Sidney H. Stein affirmed an order requiring OpenAI to produce 20 million de-identified ChatGPT logs, about 0.5% of logs; plaintiffs had asked for 120 million. De-identification plus a protective order were found adequate — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/openai-must-turn-over-20-million-chatgpt-logs-judge-affirms); [Data Privacy + Cybersecurity Insider](https://www.dataprivacyandsecurityinsider.com/2026/01/when-chats-become-evidence-court-affirms-order-requiring-openai-to-produce-20-million-de-identified-chatgpt-logs/) [secondary, search-summary]

#### Anthropic (API, Claude Enterprise, Claude Code)
- Standard commercial retention: Claude Code on Team, Enterprise and API has a "Standard: 30-day retention period." Anthropic does not train generative models on code or prompts sent under commercial terms unless the customer opts in, for example through the Development Partner Program, which exists only on the first-party API, not Bedrock or Google — [Claude Code data usage](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/data-usage)
- ZDR: "Anthropic does not store customer prompts or responses at rest after the API response is returned." It is requested through sales and enabled per organization. "Enablement does not automatically extend to other organizations under the same account" — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)
- ZDR covers the Messages and Token Counting APIs for eligible features. It covers Claude Code with Commercial-org API keys, or through Claude Enterprise with ZDR enabled; metrics/productivity data is exempt. It also covers Claude Platform on AWS on request — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)
- ZDR does NOT cover: the Claude Console/playground; Managed Agents; consumer plans; the Claude Team and Enterprise chat interfaces (the only exception is Claude Code via Enterprise with ZDR); Claude for Excel; Covered Models; third-party integrations; or CORS/browser calls. Features that are not eligible are not blocked: Batch (29-day retention), Files API, code execution (container data up to 30 days), MCP connector and Agent Skills. "Using one is a choice to step outside your ZDR arrangement" — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)
- Retention regardless of arrangement: "Even with ZDR or HIPAA arrangements in place, Anthropic may retain data where required by law or where it has been flagged by Anthropic's automated trust and safety systems... Anthropic may retain inputs and outputs for up to 2 years" — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)
- **2026 change: Covered Models.** Effective June 9, 2026, "Mythos-class models" (Claude Fable 5/5.1, Mythos 5/5.1) have prompts and outputs "retained for 30 days" for safety, even for ZDR customers. This applies on Anthropic's platform, Bedrock, Google Cloud and Azure Foundry. "By default, no Anthropic personnel can read your retained conversations." Human review happens only through a controlled path, and access is recorded in a "tamper-proof log that reviewers cannot suppress or modify" — [Anthropic Privacy Center: Covered Models](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/15425996-data-retention-practices-for-covered-models)
- The API enforces this: a Fable 5 request from an org without retention enabled returns `400`, "your organization or workspace must have data retention enabled." ZDR orgs can switch on 30-day retention for a single workspace — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)
- **2026 change: Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS).** Announced Sep 2, 2026, it combines ZDR with abuse detection for Fable. Signals "are sent directly to customers so they can review." It is listed for Claude Code, Claude Enterprise, Claude Platform, Bedrock, Claude Platform on AWS, Google's Agent Platform, and Microsoft Foundry (by invitation), and launches "this fall" — [The Register, 2026-09-02](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/02/anthropic-promises-zero-data-retention-but-customers-must-check-it-worked/5293789) [secondary]
- Claude Code client-side exposure: "Claude Code clients store session transcripts locally in plaintext under `~/.claude/projects/` for 30 days by default." Transcripts sent through `/feedback` are kept 5 years. Telemetry, error reporting and `/feedback` are off by default on Bedrock, Google, Foundry and Claude Platform on AWS. The WebFetch domain check sends hostnames to api.anthropic.com whichever provider is used — [Claude Code data usage](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/data-usage)
- Compliance-side retention: the Activity Feed keeps data 6 years. Local session transcripts in the Compliance API are kept 6 years by default unless a finite org retention is set — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)

#### Microsoft Azure OpenAI / Foundry "Models sold by Azure"
- Prompts, completions, embeddings and training data "are NOT available to OpenAI or other providers", "are NOT used by providers... to improve their models", and are not used to train foundation models without permission. The models "do NOT interact with any services operated by providers... for example, OpenAI" — [Microsoft Learn: data, privacy, security (2026-05-18)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy)
- Abuse monitoring: a sample of flagged prompts and completions may be reviewed, "by automated means including by AI models such as LLMs by default, with additional reviews by human reviewers as necessary." Human reviewers are "authorized Microsoft employees" who use Secure Access Workstations and just-in-time approval. For EEA deployments, the reviewers are located in the EEA — [Microsoft Learn: data privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy); [Microsoft Learn: abuse monitoring (2026-05-13)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/concepts/abuse-monitoring)
- Modified abuse monitoring is available to customers meeting "additional Limited Access eligibility criteria" through an application form. "Some advanced Models sold by Azure may have more stringent criteria." Once approved, storage and human review stop, but "automated review may still be conducted" — [Microsoft Learn: abuse monitoring](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/concepts/abuse-monitoring)
- Customers can check approval themselves: the resource JSON shows `"ContentLogging": "false"` in the portal or through `az cognitiveservices account show` — [Microsoft Learn: data privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy)
- The current Learn pages I fetched do not give the abuse-log retention period. Earlier versions said 30 days; see Gaps.

#### AWS Bedrock
- Bedrock uses "a zero operator access (ZOA) data security model... no operators of the service can access model input or output" and "a zero data retention (ZDR) data security model... by default, Amazon Bedrock does not store model inputs or outputs" — [AWS Bedrock abuse detection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/abuse-detection.html)
- Model Deployment Accounts, one per provider per Region, are "owned and operated by the Amazon Bedrock service team. Model providers don't have any access... they don't have access to Amazon Bedrock logs or to customer prompts and completions" — [AWS Bedrock data protection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-protection.html)
- **2026 change: data retention modes.** The modes are `none`, `default`, `aws_review` and a legacy `provider_data_share`, set per Region at account or project level. `none` means "No request or response data is written to durable storage by AWS or shared with the model provider." Claude Fable 5/5.1 require `aws_review`: prompts and completions are "retained within the AWS boundary for up to 30 days and may be reviewed by AWS". "Your content is not shared with the model provider." There is no retention change for Claude models released before Fable 5 — [AWS Bedrock data retention](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-retention.html)
- OpenAI models on Bedrock (GPT-6 Astra, GPT-5.4, 5.5, 5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna, and the "Daybreak" cyber variants): "classifier-flagged traffic will be retained for up to 30 days for automated offline abuse detection. Eligible customers may request full ZDR." For Claude Fable 5/5.1, "all traffic will be retained for up to 30 days", and flagged traffic may get human review by AWS. EFS-eligible customers "will receive ZDR through December 31, 2026", and EFS "plans to enable customer-managed encryption keys and bring your own bucket" — [AWS Bedrock abuse detection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/abuse-detection.html)
- ZDR for Claude models on Bedrock "is managed by Anthropic" — [AWS Bedrock data retention](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-retention.html)

#### Google Cloud (Vertex AI, now "Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform")
- Training restriction: "Google won't use your data to train or fine-tune any AI/ML models without your prior permission or instruction". This covers GA and pre-GA managed models — [Google: Agent Platform and ZDR (updated 2026-09-24)](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/resources/zero-data-retention)
- Abuse prompt logging applies only to customers on the online GCP Terms of Service; "customers with a Google Cloud Master Agreement are exempt... by default." Prompts are logged only after classifiers flag suspicious activity, kept "up to 90 days", and are "not encrypted by Customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK)." An opt-out form is available — [Google: abuse monitoring (updated 2026-09-24)](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/abuse-monitoring)
- Other retention paths a ZDR customer must switch off or avoid:
  - In-memory caching: 24-hour TTL, not at rest, can be disabled per project.
  - Grounding with Google Search: query logs kept up to 3 days, no opt-out (use Web Grounding for Enterprise instead).
  - Grounding with Google Maps: 30 days, no opt-out.
  - Request-response logging: off by default.
  - Interactions API: `store` defaults to true.
  - Live API session resumption: 24h.
  - CodeMender session data: up to 7 days.
  
  — [Google: Agent Platform and ZDR](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/resources/zero-data-retention)
- **2026 change: Advanced AI Safety Addendum.** For models designated "Advanced AI", accepting the addendum is Customer's instruction to "store outside Customer's Account... prompts and Generated Output, and... retain such Customer Data for up to thirty (30) days and... review it using automated tools or, as necessary, human review." This applies "Regardless of any previous elections or permissions made or given by Customer" — [Google Cloud Advanced AI Safety Addendum](https://cloud.google.com/terms/advanced-ai-safety-addendum)
- Advanced AI scope includes Claude Mythos and Claude Fable (all versions), plus Opus ≥4.7 and Sonnet ≥5 when used in Anthropic's Cyber Verification Program. These logs are "not encrypted by... CMEK", and "Zero data retention may not be possible." "To use Claude Fable 5 on Google Cloud and Mythos 5, as required by Anthropic, you must enable sharing this data with Anthropic for abuse monitoring." Consent is given per project and can be blocked through IAM (`aiplatform.consents.update`) — [Google: abuse monitoring](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/abuse-monitoring)

### Inferences
- In 2026 the privacy ceiling depends on the model. Retention is ZDR-capable for mainstream models (Opus 5.5 and earlier, GPT-5.x on most channels, Gemini). It is mandatory at about 30 days for the top "covered/Advanced AI" tier, and the pull between capability and privacy is now written into contracts.
- The same Claude Fable model is handled differently depending on the channel. On Bedrock, content is kept by AWS and not shared with Anthropic. On Google, content must be shared with Anthropic. On Foundry, Fable is available only "Hosted on Anthropic". Channel choice is therefore a material privacy decision for a bank, not just a procurement one.
- An enterprise contract (ChatGPT Enterprise, or ZDR on the API) was what kept data out of the NYT preservation order. That makes ZDR a legal-exposure control as well as a privacy control.
- OpenAI's PSP and Anthropic's EFS both answer banks' refusal of mandatory retention. Neither has independent verification yet: PSP's technical white paper was promised for September 2026, and EFS launches in fall 2026.

### Gaps
- The current Azure Learn pages do not state the abuse-log retention period (historically 30 days). I could not confirm it for 2026.
- The PSP developer guide and white paper (Sep 2026) were not retrieved. The exact definition of "safety signals" and who holds the keys in the OpenAI-storage option are unverified.
- The Anthropic Privacy Center page on standard commercial retention (30 days) was seen only in search summaries. The Claude Code doc gives the same 30-day figure for commercial users.
- OpenAI's direct terms for the ChatGPT Enterprise "Codex"/agent surfaces under ZDR were not retrieved.

## 2. Private-deployment options: residency, private networking, CMK/BYOK, dedicated capacity, sovereign and on-prem

### Takeaway
Residency and private networking are mature. Azure offers Regional, Data Zone and Global deployments. OpenAI offers 10 storage regions, with inference residency in the US, EEA and UAE. Anthropic's first-party API offers only "us" or "global" inference. Bedrock and Google pin inference to the endpoint Region. Customer-managed keys exist everywhere but mostly protect stored state, not inference. True on-prem or air-gapped frontier models are offered only by Google (Gemini on Google Distributed Cloud) and Microsoft (large models on Foundry Local, qualified customers only). No on-prem offering from OpenAI or Anthropic was found. The AWS European Sovereign Cloud launched in January 2026 but, as of September 2026, offered only open-weight models on Bedrock.

### Cited Findings

#### Residency and geography
- OpenAI API data residency regions: US, Europe (EEA + Switzerland), Australia, Canada, Japan, India, Singapore, South Korea, UK and UAE. Only the US and Europe support both storage and processing; "most other regions support storage only." Non-US regions require "approval for abuse monitoring controls, and execute a Modified Retention amendment"; UAE needs additional approval — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
- ChatGPT data residency covers the same 10 regions for eligible API customers and *new* ChatGPT Enterprise/Edu workspaces. Inference residency ("GPU execution") is available only for Europe (EEA + CH), the US and the UAE. "Non-GPU processing may still occur globally" (auth, routing, analytics, PDF text extraction). Workspace metadata, billing, logins, and data sent to Apps/MCP or web search can sit outside the region — [OpenAI Help: data and inference residency](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9903489-data-residency-and-inference-residency-for-chatgpt)
- Anthropic first-party API: `inference_geo` is either `"global"` (the default, any geography) or `"us"`. Workspace geo, which covers storage at rest, "Currently, `"us"` is the only available" option and cannot be changed after creation. Admins can restrict workspaces with `allowed_inference_geos`. The response reports `usage.inference_geo` — [Anthropic data residency](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/data-residency)
- Claude Platform on AWS: the AWS region of the workspace "does not pin where model inference runs"; inference is US or Global. "For workspaces created before September 18, 2026... Anthropic might process requests, store data, and run inference outside AWS... subservices might change without notice" — [Claude Platform on AWS](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/claude-platform-on-aws)
- Azure: in standard deployments, prompts and responses are processed within the customer's geography. A "Global" deployment "may be processed in any geography where the relevant model... is deployed". A "DataZone" deployment stays in the US or the EU member states. Data at rest, including the abuse-monitoring store, stays in the customer's geography — [Microsoft Learn: data privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy). Deployment types: Global, Data Zone (EU or US), and Regional ("up to 27 regions") — [Azure OpenAI pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/azure-openai/)
- Claude in Microsoft Foundry has two hosting options:
  - "Hosted on Azure": "prompts and completions remain within Azure. Only usage metadata and content flagged by Anthropic's safety systems egress to Anthropic". Global or US Data Zone.
  - "Hosted on Anthropic": routes to Anthropic infrastructure, Global only. Fable and Mythos are available only here.
  - "Anthropic acts as an independent processor for Microsoft."
  
  — [Claude in Microsoft Foundry](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/claude-in-microsoft-foundry)
- Bedrock: when cross-region inference is on, retained data "are stored in destination Regions" — [AWS Bedrock data retention](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-retention.html). On Bedrock and Google, the inference region is set by the endpoint or inference profile — [Anthropic data residency](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/data-residency)
- Google abuse and Advanced AI logs are stored "in the same region or multi-region selected by the customer" and are covered by Data Residency, Access Transparency and VPC Service Controls (VPC-SC) — [Google: abuse monitoring](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/abuse-monitoring)

#### Private networking and keys
- Anthropic: AWS PrivateLink is supported for Claude Platform on AWS. CMEK uses AWS KMS keys only, in the same account and region as the workspace — [Claude Platform on AWS](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/claude-platform-on-aws). Claude Enterprise includes CMEK, "Provision an encryption key in your own cloud provider", and a US-only inference option — [Claude Help: Enterprise plan](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9797531-what-is-the-enterprise-plan)
- Claude Code encryption at rest by provider: Anthropic API uses AES-256 disk encryption (or ZDR); Bedrock uses AWS-managed keys with optional KMS CMK; Google uses Google-managed keys with optional CMEK — [Claude Code data usage](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/data-usage)
- OpenAI Enterprise Key Management (EKM) encrypts *application state* with customer keys from AWS KMS, GCP or Azure Key Vault. It excludes Assistants and vision fine-tuning — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data). Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+); SAML SSO is supported — [OpenAI Enterprise privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/)
- Azure stored-feature data (Responses API, Assistants threads, stored completions, files) is kept in the customer's tenant and geography, AES-256 by default, "with the option of using a customer managed key." "Microsoft-managed keys are always used to ensure baseline encryption." Some preview features do not support CMK. Resources can be restricted to an Azure Virtual Network — [Microsoft Learn: data privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy); [Claude in Microsoft Foundry](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/claude-in-microsoft-foundry)
- Bedrock: VPC and PrivateLink, KMS, CloudTrail and FIPS 140-3 endpoints. Data-retention mode can be enforced org-wide with an SCP: Deny `bedrock-mantle:PutAccountDataRetention` unless `DataRetentionMode = none` — [AWS Bedrock data protection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-protection.html); [AWS Bedrock data retention](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-retention.html)
- Gemini Enterprise Standard/Plus includes "VPC-SC and Customer-Managed Encryption Keys" and "sovereign data boundaries for your data residency needs" — [Google Cloud: Gemini Enterprise](https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise)

#### Dedicated capacity
- Azure Provisioned Throughput Units (PTUs) give "predictable costs, with monthly and annual reservations available" — [Azure OpenAI pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/azure-openai/)
- Anthropic has Priority Tier committed tokens per minute (TPM). US-only inference burns 1.1 tokens of commitment per token — [Anthropic data residency](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/data-residency). Claude Platform on AWS "uses a separate capacity pool" from the first-party API and Bedrock — [Claude Platform on AWS](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/claude-platform-on-aws)

#### Sovereign, government and on-prem
- **Google Distributed Cloud (GDC).** Gemini on GDC air-gapped became GA on Aug 28, 2025. GDC air-gapped is "authorized for US Government Secret and Top Secret missions", on NVIDIA Blackwell — [Google Cloud press, 2025-08-28](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2025-08-28-Google-Cloud-Makes-Gemini-Everywhere-Vision-a-Reality,-Doubles-Down-on-Enterprise-AI-Commitment-to-Singapore) [search-summary]. At Next '26 (Apr 22, 2026), Google announced the "latest Gemini Flash models" in preview for GDC connected on HGX B200/B300. GDC air-gapped runs on "purpose-built, Google-supplied hardware"; GDC connected runs on customer hardware — [Google Cloud blog: GDC at Next '26](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/hybrid-cloud/google-distributed-cloud-at-next26)
- **Microsoft.** On Feb 24, 2026, Microsoft announced Azure Local disconnected operations and M365 Local (available worldwide), and "Foundry Local" for "large models... available to qualified customers" on NVIDIA GPUs. The post does not name which model vendors can run disconnected — [Microsoft blog, 2026-02-24](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/02/24/microsoft-sovereign-cloud-adds-governance-productivity-and-support-for-large-ai-models-securely-running-even-when-completely-disconnected/)

GPT-4o was approved for Top Secret use in Azure — [FedScoop](https://fedscoop.com/openais-gpt-4o-gets-green-light-for-top-secret-use-in-microsofts-azure-cloud/) [search-summary]

## 3. Confidential computing for LLM inference

### Takeaway
Confidential GPUs exist as IaaS on Azure (NCCadsH100v5) and Google (A3 H100 Confidential VMs, with Blackwell and G4 in preview). They let a bank run its *own* model under a hardware trusted execution environment (TEE) with remote attestation, protected from the cloud's hypervisor and operators. However, as of mid-2026 no frontier *proprietary* LLM API (GPT, Claude, Gemini) is offered as confidential inference to enterprise customers. Microsoft's productized confidential inferencing covers only the Azure OpenAI Whisper model. Anthropic's work is a 2025 research sketch. AWS relies on a "zero operator access" design claim rather than customer-verifiable attestation of the model service.

### Cited Findings
- Azure confidential GPU VMs (NCCadsH100v5) combine AMD SEV-SNP with H100 confidential computing into "a unified Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) across the CPU and GPU". Data inside is "protected from unauthorized access or modification by any code outside the environment, including privileged Microsoft code such as virtualization host operating system and Hyper-V hypervisor" — [Microsoft Tech Community: confidential inferencing deep-dive](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azureconfidentialcomputingblog/azure-ai-confidential-inferencing-technical-deep-dive/4253150) [search-summary]; product listed at [Microsoft Learn: Azure confidential computing products (2026-06-10)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/confidential-computing/overview-azure-products)
- The June 2026 Learn catalog lists only "Confidential inferencing with the Azure OpenAI Whisper model", with "TEEs, encrypted prompt protection, user anonymity, and OHTTP". No GPT-model confidential inferencing is listed — [Microsoft Learn: Azure confidential computing products](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/confidential-computing/overview-azure-products)
- The mechanism: clients encrypt prompts with HPKE over Oblivious HTTP. Requests pass through Azure Front Door and the Azure OpenAI load balancer to OHTTP gateways inside confidential GPU VMs. The gateways obtain HPKE private keys from a KMS only after presenting a Microsoft Azure Attestation token that satisfies the key-release policy — [Microsoft Tech Community: deep-dive](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azureconfidentialcomputingblog/azure-ai-confidential-inferencing-technical-deep-dive/4253150) [search-summary]
- Google Confidential Computing: "Confidential VMs on the accelerator-optimized A3 machine series with NVIDIA H100 GPUs." Confidential Space keeps data "protected from all parties—including the underlying infrastructure and their own operators." The product page notes some listed items are in preview — [Google Cloud: Confidential Computing](https://cloud.google.com/security/products/confidential-computing)
- Google announced Confidential VMs and Confidential GKE Nodes on G4 (NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell) in preview, and Blackwell confidential computing for Gemini on GDC — [Google Cloud blog: Confidential Computing news](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/identity-security/verifiable-trust-in-the-ai-era-whats-new-in-confidential-computing) [search-summary]. Google's June 11, 2026 "Confidential AI" post covers its collaboration with Apple's Private Cloud Compute on Intel TDX plus NVIDIA Blackwell. It does not announce confidential Gemini inference for Cloud customers — [Google Cloud blog, 2026-06-11](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/identity-security/powering-the-next-era-of-confidential-ai)
- AWS Bedrock claims "zero operator access (ZOA)", and in ESC, "No service operators can access model input or output during inference." These are AWS statements, and the fetched docs do not describe any customer-side attestation — [AWS Bedrock abuse detection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/abuse-detection.html); [AWS Security blog, 2026-09-17](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/run-open-weight-models-on-aws-bedrock-in-aws-european-sovereign-cloud/)
- Anthropic's "Confidential Inference via Trusted Virtual Machines" (June 18, 2025) sets two goals: protecting model weights and proving user data is readable only inside verifiable servers. It is explicitly "just a sketch of our research... too soon to forecast how it will evolve into specific designs or features" — [Anthropic research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/confidential-inference-trusted-vms)
- OpenAI's closest offering is PSP storage "encrypted using keys controlled by the customer" so personnel "cannot access the underlying content". That protects *stored* content for safety analysis, not inference execution — [OpenAI: Offering ZDR for frontier models](https://openai.com/index/offering-zero-data-retention-for-frontier-models/)

### Inferences
- Confidential computing protects against the cloud host and its operators: hypervisor, host OS, physical memory access and insiders without attested code. It does not protect against the *model operator's own attested code*. If the attested inference stack logs prompts, the TEE does not stop it; attestation only proves which code runs.
- For a bank, the confidential-GPU route is realistic in 2026 for open-weight models the bank runs itself. It is not available for GPT, Claude or Gemini APIs, with the partial exception of Gemini on GDC, where the customer controls the hardware perimeter.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Azure confidential GPU VMs have newer SKUs beyond H100 (H200 or Blackwell) as GA.
- The AWS Nitro and GPU confidential-computing specifics for Bedrock inference were not retrieved.
- The status of the Azure AI Confidential Inferencing preview beyond Whisper (for example, any GPT models) was not found in the 2026 catalog.

## 6. What these offerings do NOT solve

### Takeaway
Every offering above still decrypts prompts in plaintext on provider-controlled infrastructure at inference time. No-training, ZDR, deletion windows and human-review limits are mostly contractual and can only be audited indirectly. Several structural gaps remain even with ZDR:
- legal holds and court orders
- retention of flagged content (up to 2 years at Anthropic)
- CSAM carve-outs
- stateful features outside ZDR
- mandatory retention for the most capable models
- abuse logs not covered by CMEK
- US jurisdiction (CLOUD Act) over US providers' EU data
- plaintext transcripts left on developer machines

### Cited Findings
- **Plaintext processing.** Azure evaluates prompts "in real time for harmful content"; the models are "stateless", but the service processes the content — [Microsoft Learn: data privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy). Anthropic's ZDR means no storage "at rest after the API response is returned", which implies in-memory processing in the clear — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention). The only productized confidential LLM-adjacent inference is Whisper on Azure (see §3) — [Microsoft Learn: confidential products](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/confidential-computing/overview-azure-products)
- **Legal holds and litigation.**
  - OpenAI had to retain consumer and non-ZDR API content under court order from May to Sep 26, 2025, and still holds April–September 2025 data — [OpenAI: response to NYT](https://openai.com/index/response-to-nyt-data-demands/)
  - 20 million de-identified ChatGPT logs were ordered produced (affirmed Jan 5, 2026) — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/openai-must-turn-over-20-million-chatgpt-logs-judge-affirms) [search-summary]
  - Anthropic and OpenAI both reserve retention "where required by law" even under ZDR — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention); [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
- **Government access and jurisdiction.** Under oath before the French Senate on June 10, 2025, Microsoft France's legal director said Microsoft "cannot guarantee" French public-sector data would be protected from US authorities' requests without local consent. He added that no such demand had occurred so far — [The Register, 2025-07-25](https://www.theregister.com/off-prem/2025/07/25/microsoft-exec-admits-it-cannot-guarantee-data-sovereignty/458553) [secondary, search-summary]
- **Flagged-content retention and carve-outs.**
  - Anthropic keeps flagged inputs and outputs up to 2 years even under ZDR or HIPAA — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)
  - OpenAI keeps CSAM-flagged images even under ZDR — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
  - Bedrock may store and review CSAM-flagged inputs and report them to NCMEC — [AWS Bedrock abuse detection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/abuse-detection.html)
- **Human and third-party access.**
  - OpenAI API data may be reviewed by "specialized third-party contractors" for abuse — [OpenAI Enterprise privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/)
  - On Azure, Microsoft employees may review flagged content unless the customer has modified abuse monitoring — [Microsoft Learn: abuse monitoring](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/concepts/abuse-monitoring)
  - Google employees may assess flagged prompts — [Google: abuse monitoring](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/abuse-monitoring)
  - Human review of covered models goes through a logged path — [Anthropic Privacy Center: Covered Models](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/15425996-data-retention-practices-for-covered-models)
- **Customer keys do not cover everything.** Google abuse and Advanced AI logs are "not encrypted by Customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK)" — [Google: abuse monitoring](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/abuse-monitoring). OpenAI EKM excludes Assistants and vision fine-tuning — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
- **ZDR scope holes.**
  - Anthropic: Batch, Files, code execution, MCP connector, Skills, Managed Agents, and the Claude Enterprise chat UI are outside ZDR. Non-eligible features are *not blocked* under ZDR — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)
  - OpenAI: Assistants, Files, Vector stores, Batches and Conversations are outside ZDR — [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
  - Google: Search grounding logs (3 days) and Maps grounding (30 days) cannot be disabled — [Google: Agent Platform and ZDR](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/resources/zero-data-retention)
  - Residency: third-party integrations (Apps, MCP, web search) and CPU-side processing can leave the region even with inference residency — [OpenAI Help: residency](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9903489-data-residency-and-inference-residency-for-chatgpt)
- **Mandatory retention for the strongest models.** Anthropic Covered Models: 30 days even for ZDR — [Anthropic Privacy Center](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/15425996-data-retention-practices-for-covered-models). Google Advanced AI Addendum: 30 days, applied "Regardless of any previous elections" — [Google AASA](https://cloud.google.com/terms/advanced-ai-safety-addendum). Bedrock `aws_review` for Fable — [AWS Bedrock data retention](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-retention.html)
- **Verification is mostly self-attested.**
  - Azure exposes a `ContentLogging=false` attribute as the customer's check that abuse storage is off. This is a configuration flag, not a cryptographic proof — [Microsoft Learn: data privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy)
  - Anthropic returns `usage.inference_geo` per response — [Anthropic data residency](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/data-residency)
  - The Register's headline on EFS is that "customers must check it worked" — [The Register, 2026-09-02](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/02/anthropic-promises-zero-data-retention-but-customers-must-check-it-worked/5293789)
  - Claude Platform on AWS warns "subservices might change without notice", and older workspaces may run outside AWS — [Claude Platform on AWS](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/claude-platform-on-aws)
- **Endpoint-side leakage.** Claude Code keeps plaintext transcripts under `~/.claude/projects/` for 30 days by default. `/feedback` uploads include code and are kept 5 years, unless disabled — [Claude Code data usage](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/data-usage)
- **Technically enforced controls (the exceptions).**
  - Bedrock `data_retention_mode: none` makes retention-requiring models unavailable, and it can be forced org-wide with an SCP — [AWS Bedrock data retention](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-retention.html)
  - Anthropic HIPAA-enabled orgs get `400` on non-covered features, and Covered Models return `400` without retention — [Anthropic API and data retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)
  - Anthropic workspace `allowed_inference_geos` rejects out-of-policy geos — [Anthropic data residency](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/data-residency)
  - Google caching can be switched off per project through an API, and Advanced AI consent can be blocked through IAM — [Google: Agent Platform and ZDR](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/resources/zero-data-retention); [Google: abuse monitoring](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/abuse-monitoring)

### Inferences
- **What is technically enforced** is *policy gating*: which features, models and regions a request may use. **What is contractual** is what happens to plaintext once it reaches the provider (no training, no storage, deletion, who may look). Only hardware TEEs with customer-verified attestation would turn the second category into the first, and no frontier API offers that in 2026.
- For a bank, the remaining risks after buying the strongest tier (ZDR, CMEK, residency, private link) are:
  - provider insider access to live traffic or flagged content
  - compelled disclosure under US law, or litigation holds on anything that is stored
  - provider breach while data is transient or retained
  - silent scope drift, where new features or models fall outside ZDR and residency by default ("If OpenAI releases any new features or modalities, they will not be data residency eligible unless listed" — [OpenAI Help: residency](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9903489-data-residency-and-inference-residency-for-chatgpt))
  - client-side copies such as local transcripts and logs
- The 2026 retention requirements for frontier models make the trade-off explicit: the best models are the ones least compatible with "nothing stored". This is a structural argument for keeping the most sensitive material (secrets, PII, customer records) local and sending the frontier model only redacted or minimized context.
